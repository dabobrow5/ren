Your privacy

What Ren holds, who helps, and what will never be done with it.

This is the privacy policy for Ren, the app, at renishere.com. Ren is made by Light Alchemy Labs, a DBA of Light Alchemy Ventures LLC. Both names refer to the same business, called “we” and “us” here.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

When you share something with Ren, it stays with you.

Your conversations, memories, and personal history are stored privately under your own account. The system itself enforces this: no other person using Ren can reach your data. That is checked on every single request. The only things another person ever sees are the things you choose to send them, like a Spark, or what you say in a Heart-to-Heart you join together.

The people who build Ren do not read your conversations or your memories, and access is tightly restricted to protect them. That commitment is also built into the system: what you share with Ren is encrypted (AES-256) under a key that belongs to your account alone before it is stored. Your key is itself locked by a master key held in Google Cloud’s key management service, kept apart from the database, and every use of it is logged. So what you share reads as ciphertext to anyone looking at the database or a backup, including the people who build Ren.

This is not end-to-end encryption. So that Ren can remember you and answer you, Ren’s servers unlock your key for the moments it takes to do that, and the companies named below receive what they need to do their part. A few things the system needs in order to run, such as account identifiers, dates and times, settings, and whether you subscribe, are stored without this extra layer, protected by our access controls and by Google Cloud’s own encryption at rest.

Your data is never sold, never licensed, and never used for advertising or marketing of any kind. It is shared only with the companies named in this policy, and only so they can run their part of Ren for you. It exists for one purpose: to help Ren know you better and meet you more fully.

Ren remembers you so Ren can walk alongside you. That memory belongs to you.

What Ren collects, how, and why

Only what you bring, and what it takes to hold it for you. Everything below is linked to your account.

  • Who you are to Ren. Your name and the email address you sign in with (from Apple, Google, or the email you sign in with), an account number the system gives you, and, if you give them, the name you would like to be called, your pronouns, your language, and your time zone (so Ren never guesses the time of day). Used to sign you in and to address you the way you asked.
  • What you share with Ren. What you type or say to Ren, the memories Ren forms from it, journal entries, habits and their notes, Stillness sits, Purpose, your Journey, and what you tell Ren while preparing for or reflecting on a Heart-to-Heart. If you photograph a handwritten journal page, the photo is read so its words can be saved; the photo itself is not kept. Used so Ren can answer you and remember you.
  • Ren’s understanding of how you are. Gentle reads of your wellbeing, drawn by the system from what you share, so Ren can meet you where you are and notice when something is hard. See “Your health information” below.
  • What you share with other people. Sparks and Ripples (short videos, with sound, that you record and send to someone or to a circle), their titles, your connections and circles, and the words spoken or typed in a Heart-to-Heart you join with someone. Used only to deliver them to the people you chose.
  • Email addresses of people you invite. If you invite someone to a Heart-to-Heart or a circle by typing their email address, we keep that address with the invitation. It is used only to match the invitation to their Ren account, if they have one, so they can see it and answer it. We never email that address, never add it to any list, and never tell you whether the person uses Ren. A circle invitation’s address is removed when it is answered or the circle ends, and every invitation goes when you or they delete your account.
  • If you turn on texting. Your mobile number, that you confirmed it, the exact consent sentence you agreed to with its date, and the texts Ren sends you and you send back. Used only so Ren can reach you and you can answer.
  • If you subscribe. Which plan you have and whether it is active. Apple handles payment; we never see your card. Used to give you what you paid for.
  • Your device. A token so your phone can receive Ren’s notifications, and an installation identifier that Firebase uses to deliver them. Used only to deliver notifications.
  • How much of Ren you use. A daily count per person of conversations, sits, voice, and the cost of providing them, plus any feedback you give on a reply and any 0 to 10 rating you choose to give. It counts and never records what was said. Used to keep use fair within your plan, and to understand how Ren is used, in totals and per person, inside our own small team. Never for advertising, and never shared.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service: what the app was doing in code when it failed, the app version, your device model and iOS version, and a random identifier for the installation. It contains nothing you have written or said, and we do not attach your name or email to it. Used only to find and fix what broke.
  • When you contact us. Anything you send in a bug report (with the app version, device, and iOS version, so we can reproduce it), a safety report, or an email to support. Used only to answer you and fix the problem, or to act on the report.
  • The waiting list. If you ask on renishere.com to be told when Ren is open to you, we keep the email address and anything else you put in that form, plus the network address and browser type it came from, to keep the form free of spam. Used only to invite you.

There is no advertising identifier, no tracking across other apps or websites, no data broker, and no advertising or marketing analytics. Crash reports, described above, are the only diagnostics that leave the app on their own.

What stays on your devices

  • Apple Health. Only if you allow it. Ren on your Apple Watch adds the minutes of a breathing practice to Mindful Minutes in Apple Health. That is all Ren does with Apple Health in this version: Ren does not read your heart rate, and Ren on your iPhone neither reads from nor writes to Apple Health. Your health data from Apple Health is never sent to us, never disclosed to anyone else, never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and never stored in iCloud. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app or your Watch’s settings.
  • Your calendar. If you allow it, the nightstand shows the next thing you have. It is read on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Your voice. When you speak to Ren instead of typing, your iPhone turns speech into words using Apple’s speech recognition, which may use Apple’s servers under Apple’s own privacy terms. Ren receives the words, not the recording. When you record a Spark, your phone hears its first words the same way, and Anthropic’s AI, on our servers, may tidy them into a suggested title.

The AI companies that help Ren answer you

Ren is an AI, and runs on AI made by two other companies. Before anything you share is sent to them, Ren tells you about them and asks for your agreement.

  • Anthropic makes the AI Ren runs on. It receives what Ren needs to answer you: your messages, journal entries, photos of handwritten pages, what you say in a Heart-to-Heart, your replies to Ren’s texts, the first words of a Spark (for its title), words you have written when you switch Ren’s language, and the context Ren holds about you, such as your name and what Ren remembers, so it can answer in context. Under its commercial terms Anthropic may not train its AI on any of it, and keeps it only for a limited time under its own retention policy.
  • ElevenLabs turns Ren’s replies into Ren’s spoken voice. It receives the words Ren says aloud, which can include things you have told Ren. It never receives your voice.

You can take that agreement back at any time in Ren, under You, then Settings. From then on nothing you share is sent to Anthropic or ElevenLabs, which means Ren cannot reply or speak until you agree again. Crisis resources, your settings, and deleting your account stay open either way.

Who else helps us run Ren

  • Google Cloud and Firebase host Ren’s servers, database, stored files, and encryption keys, handle sign-in and notifications, and receive crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Google also hosts our support email.
  • Twilio sends and receives text messages and phone confirmation codes, only if you turn texting on.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, notifications, speech recognition, and the App Store, which handles your subscription and payment.
  • Google (if you sign in with Google) and Apple (if you sign in with Apple) confirm who you are. If you choose Hide My Email, we only ever see Apple’s private relay address.

Every company named in this policy that receives your data is bound, by its contract with us or by its own terms, to protect it at least as well as this policy does: to use it only to provide its part of Ren, never to sell it, and never to use it for advertising. Nobody else receives your data.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. If Light Alchemy Ventures LLC is ever sold or merged, your data would move only under this same promise, and we would tell you first.

Your health information

Some of what you share with Ren is about your health and wellbeing: how you are feeling, what you are carrying, how you are sleeping or coping. Ren also forms gentle reads of how you are, to meet you well and notice when something is hard. Some laws, including Washington’s My Health My Data Act, call this consumer health data.

  • Why we collect it: only so Ren can respond to you, remember you, and offer crisis resources when something serious surfaces.
  • Where it comes from: only from you, through what you share in Ren. Nothing from Apple Health is part of it.
  • Who receives it: only the companies named above, to provide Ren to you. Never sold, never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and never shared with other people using Ren unless you send it to them yourself.
  • Your consent: we ask for it when you first meet Ren. You can withdraw it in Settings as described above, or by deleting your account.
  • Your rights: you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent, by writing to support@lightalchemylabs.com. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Ren is not a medical service and this information is not a medical record. It is not covered by HIPAA.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and what you share with Ren: for as long as you have an account, so Ren can remember you. You can see, change, or delete what Ren remembers at any time, on You.
  • Sparks and Ripples: one year, unless someone keeps one. You are told a week before a Spark expires.
  • Invitations: as described above, until answered, until the circle ends, or until either account is deleted.
  • Backups: daily backups, kept for 98 days, so Ren can be restored if something goes wrong. They are protected and are never used for anything else.

Deleting everything

Inside Ren, open You, then Settings, and choose Delete my account. It asks you to confirm in two steps, and tells you exactly what will be lost before you agree to lose it. Then everything goes: conversations, memories, journal entries, habits, sits, Purpose, your Journey, your phone number and texting consent, invitations you sent, bug reports you sent us, and your sign-in itself. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to end Ren’s access to your Apple ID. Two things are kept, without your content: a beta agreement, if you signed one, and, if you reported someone, a record that the report was made, with your name removed, so a pattern of harm is not erased.

Sparks and Ripples you sent, and your words in a shared Heart-to-Heart, are deleted with your account, and disappear for the other person too. The other person keeps their own words and their own reflections, because those are theirs.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

After deletion, the last copies in our backups are gone within 98 days. After that your data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. That is the intent, not a limitation.

Your choices and rights

You can see and remove what Ren remembers inside the app; turn off what Ren learns from, texting, notifications, the calendar, and Apple Health at any time; take back your agreement to the AI companies in Settings; and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to get a copy of it. Write to support@lightalchemylabs.com and a person will answer within 30 days. We will never treat you differently for asking.

Children

Ren is for adults. It is not directed at, or intended for, anyone under 18, and you must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using Ren, write to us and we will delete the account.

Ren is an AI, and not a crisis service

Ren is an AI, not a person: a companion, not a therapist, a doctor, or an emergency service. When something serious surfaces, Ren says so plainly and offers real crisis resources: call or text 988, text HOME to 741741, or call 911. If you are in danger, please contact them directly.

Text messages

Light Alchemy Labs, a DBA of Light Alchemy Ventures LLC and the maker of Ren, is the only sender. Ren can text you, but only if you ask. You add your number yourself, you agree in a separate and deliberate step with a box that starts empty, and you confirm the number with a one-time code before anything can be sent to it. Nothing about signing up opts you in, and agreeing is not required to use Ren.

The messages are personal and one-to-one, about something you have already discussed with Ren. They are never promotional or marketing, and never sent on behalf of anyone else. When you text Ren back, Ren reads your reply and may answer it once.

Your mobile number and the fact that you agreed to be texted are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for any purpose, including marketing, promotion, or lead generation. No advertisers, no data brokers, no affiliates, no other users, no exceptions. It is used for one thing: so Ren can reach you, and so you can reach Ren back. Twilio carries the messages for us and may not use your number for anything else.

Ren writes when something matters. Never more than four messages in any seven days, and never two in one day, limits enforced by the system itself, not by good intentions. Only during daytime hours where you live. Ren does not send campaigns, newsletters, promotions, or reminders to come back to the app.

Message frequency: up to 4 messages a week. Never two in one day. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Reply STOP at any time and Ren stops, immediately and without asking why. Reply HELP and you will get a way to reach a person. You can also turn it off in your settings, which is the same thing by a quieter door.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in Ren before the change takes effect. We will never use what you have already shared in a new way without asking you first.
