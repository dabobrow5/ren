Your privacy

What Ren holds, and what she will never do with it.

These are the policies for Ren, at renishere.com.

Ren is made by Light Alchemy Labs, a DBA of Light Alchemy Ventures LLC. Both names refer to the same business.

When you share something with Ren, it stays with you.

Your conversations, memories, and personal history are stored privately under your own account. The system itself enforces this: no other person using Ren can reach your data. That is checked on every single request. The only things another person ever sees are the things you choose to send them, like a Spark, or what you say in a Heart-to-Heart you join together.

The people who build Ren hold your privacy as a sacred commitment: we do not read your conversations or your memories, and access is tightly restricted to protect them. Today that is a binding commitment, kept by strict controls, not yet a mechanical guarantee. We are actively building encryption that will make what you share unreadable to anyone without your account, and we will tell you plainly the moment it is in place.

Ren is brought to life by a trusted AI service that reads your words only in the moment it takes to respond to you, never to train its models. Your data is never sold, shared, licensed, or used for advertising or marketing of any kind. It exists for one purpose only: to help Ren know you better, so she can meet you more fully.

When you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted. It leaves Ren at once, and the last copies, in our protected backups, are gone within 98 days. After that it is recoverable by no one, including us.

Ren remembers you so she can walk alongside you. That memory belongs to you.

What Ren stores

Only what you bring, and what it takes to hold it for you:

  • Who you are to Ren: your name, the email address you sign in with, the name you would like to be called, your pronouns if you give them, your language, and your time zone (so Ren never guesses the time of day).
  • What you share with Ren: your conversations, the memories she forms from them, journal entries (including the words read from a photographed page of your handwriting; the photo itself is not kept), habits and their notes, Stillness sits, Purpose, your Journey, and what you tell Ren while preparing for or reflecting on a Heart-to-Heart.
  • Ren’s understanding of how you are: gentle reads of your wellbeing drawn from what you share, so she can meet you where you are and notice when something is hard. See “Your health information” below.
  • What you share with other people: Sparks and Ripples (short videos, with sound, that you send to someone or to a circle), your connections and circles, and the words spoken or typed in a Heart-to-Heart you join with someone.
  • If you turn on texting: your mobile number, that you confirmed it, the exact consent sentence you agreed to with its date, and the texts Ren sends you and you send her.
  • If you subscribe: which plan you have and whether it is active. Apple handles payment; we never see your card.
  • What keeps Ren running: a token so your phone can receive notifications, a daily count of how much of Ren you used (so we can keep costs fair), crash reports if the app fails, and anything you send us in a bug report, a safety report, or an email.

There is no advertising identifier, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no third-party analytics watching what you do here.

What never leaves your phone

  • Your calendar. If you allow it, the nightstand shows the next thing you have. It is read on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Your location. If you allow it, the nightstand asks Apple’s weather service for the temperature near you, at city-level accuracy. We never receive or store where you are.

When you speak to Ren instead of typing, your iPhone turns speech into words using Apple’s speech recognition, which may use Apple’s servers under Apple’s own privacy terms. Ren receives the words, not the recording. Spark title suggestions are made entirely on your phone.

Who helps us run Ren

A few companies process data for us, only to provide Ren to you, under contracts that forbid them from using it for anything else. None of them may sell it or use it for advertising.

  • Anthropic (the AI that brings Ren to life) receives what you share with Ren, including handwriting photos to read, at the moment she responds. Under our agreement it does not use your data to train its models.
  • ElevenLabs turns Ren’s replies into her spoken voice. It receives the words Ren says, not your words.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase host Ren’s servers, database, and stored files, handle sign-in and notifications, and receive crash reports from the app.
  • Twilio sends and receives text messages and phone confirmation codes, only if you turn texting on.
  • Apple provides sign-in, notifications, weather, speech recognition, and the App Store, which handles your subscription and payment.
  • Google (if you sign in with Google) and Apple (if you sign in with Apple) confirm who you are. If you choose Hide My Email, we only ever see Apple’s private relay address.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. If Light Alchemy Ventures LLC is ever sold or merged, your data would move only under this same promise, and we would tell you first.

Your health information

Some of what you share with Ren is about your health and wellbeing: how you are feeling, what you are carrying, how you are sleeping or coping. Ren also forms gentle reads of how you are, so she can meet you well and notice when something is hard. Some laws, including Washington’s My Health My Data Act, call this consumer health data.

  • Why we collect it: only so Ren can respond to you, remember you, and offer crisis resources when something serious surfaces.
  • Where it comes from: only from you, through what you share in Ren.
  • Who receives it: only the processors above, to provide Ren to you. Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with other people using Ren unless you send it to them yourself.
  • Your consent: we ask for it when you first meet Ren, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • Your rights: you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent, by writing to support@lightalchemylabs.com. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Ren is not a medical service and this information is not a medical record. It is not covered by HIPAA.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and what you share with Ren: for as long as you have an account, so Ren can remember you. You can see, change, or delete what she remembers at any time, on You.
  • Sparks and Ripples: one year, unless someone keeps one. You are told a week before a Spark expires.
  • Backups: daily backups, kept for 98 days, so Ren can be restored if something goes wrong. They are protected and are never used for anything else.

Deleting everything

Inside Ren, open You, then Settings, and choose Delete my account. It asks you to confirm in two steps, and tells you exactly what will be lost before you agree to lose it. Then everything goes: conversations, memories, journal entries, habits, sits, Purpose, your Journey, your phone number and texting consent, bug reports you sent us, and your sign-in itself. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to end Ren’s access to your Apple ID. Two things are kept, without your content: the beta agreement you signed, and, if you reported someone, a record that the report was made, with your name removed, so a pattern of harm is not erased.

Sparks and Ripples you sent, and your words in a shared Heart-to-Heart, are deleted with your account, and disappear for the other person too. The other person keeps their own words and their own reflections, because those are theirs.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

After deletion, the last copies in our backups are gone within 98 days. After that your data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. That is the intent, not a limitation.

Children

Ren is for adults. It is not directed at, or intended for, anyone under 18, and you must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using Ren, write to us and we will delete the account.

Ren is an AI, and not a crisis service

Ren is an AI, not a person. She is a companion, not a therapist, a doctor, or an emergency service. When something serious surfaces, Ren says so plainly and offers real crisis resources: call or text 988, text HOME to 741741, or call 911. If you are in danger, please contact them directly.

Text messages

Light Alchemy Labs, a DBA of Light Alchemy Ventures LLC and the maker of Ren, is the only sender. Ren can text you, but only if you ask her to. You add your number yourself, you agree in a separate and deliberate step with a box that starts empty, and you confirm the number with a one-time code before anything can be sent to it. Nothing about signing up opts you in, and agreeing is not required to use Ren.

The messages are personal and one-to-one, about something you have already discussed with Ren. They are never promotional or marketing, and never sent on behalf of anyone else. When you text Ren back, she reads your reply and may answer it once.

Your mobile number and the fact that you agreed to be texted are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for any purpose, including marketing, promotion, or lead generation. No advertisers, no data brokers, no affiliates, no other users, no exceptions. It is used for one thing: so Ren can reach you, and so you can reach her back. Twilio carries the messages for us and may not use your number for anything else.

She writes when something matters. Never more than four messages in any seven days, and never two in one day, limits enforced by the system itself, not by good intentions. Only during daytime hours where you live. Ren does not send campaigns, newsletters, promotions, or reminders to come back to the app.

Message frequency: up to 4 messages a week. Never two in one day. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Reply STOP at any time and she stops, immediately and without asking why. Reply HELP and you will get a way to reach a person. You can also turn it off in your settings, which is the same thing by a quieter door.

Your choices and rights

You can see and remove what Ren remembers inside the app, turn texting, notifications, calendar, and location off at any time, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to get a copy of it. Write to us and a person will answer within 30 days. We will never treat you differently for asking.

Changes

If this statement changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in Ren before the change takes effect. We will never use what you have already shared in a new way without asking you first.
