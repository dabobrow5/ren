Stories · A therapist's perspective on Ren

"Something they actively take part in."

Kristine has practiced as a licensed therapist for years. She writes here about what she has seen when mindfulness, journaling, and Ren enter the work together.

In my years of practicing therapy, I've come to see mindfulness and journaling as essential parts of effective therapy, not optional extras.

Therapy asks clients to understand themselves more deeply. Yet many people come to me with their minds running on autopilot. They react before they notice, ruminate without resolution, and carry feelings they can't yet name. Insight alone rarely changes that. Clients need a way to slow down and observe their experience as it happens. Mindfulness gives them that. It builds the capacity to pause, notice a thought or sensation, and respond rather than react. I've seen it ease anxiety, soften self-criticism, and help clients stay present with emotions they once avoided. With the addition of Ren into the therapeutic process, it can teach clients how to utilize these skills to help aid them in their treatment.

Journaling does something equally important: it carries the work beyond the therapy room. A weekly session is a small part of someone's life. Writing between sessions lets clients track patterns, process difficult moments as they happen, and notice progress they might otherwise miss. Moreover, having prompts that are suggested to the client depending on where they are in their current journey through Ren helps to make the journaling process more productive and insightful.

"Writing between sessions lets clients track patterns, process difficult moments as they happen, and notice progress they might otherwise miss."

Together, with Ren these practices turn therapy from something that happens to a client into something they actively take part in. They build self-awareness, emotional regulation and self-compassion, which are the foundations of lasting change. That's why I continue to weave them into my work, adapted to each person's needs, pace and comfort and by using Ren, it will make this process much more efficient and meaningful to clients during and in between sessions.

Kristine
Licensed therapist and a member of the Light Alchemy team
