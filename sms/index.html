Only people who have created an account for Ren, our personal wellbeing app, entered their own mobile number in their profile settings, confirmed that number with a one-time passcode, and separately agreed to be texted. We do not buy, rent, import or otherwise obtain phone numbers from anywhere.

How you consent, and it is only for this

Consent is given in the app, in your profile settings, by ticking a box that is empty by default and stands on its own. The sentence reads:

“Ren may text me occasionally, up to 4 messages a month and never twice in a week, and only when something we’ve talked about genuinely warrants it. I can reply STOP at any time to end it, or HELP if I need support. Message and data rates may apply.”

What we send

Infrequent, personal, one-to-one messages about something you have already discussed with Ren inside the app: acknowledging a difficult date you mentioned, or following up on something you asked to be asked about. Conversational care, never marketing. No promotions, no offers, no advertising.

How often

Up to 4 messages per month, and never twice within a week. Most months, fewer or none. These limits are enforced by the system itself.

Your number is never shared

Your mobile number and the fact that you consented are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for any purpose, including marketing, promotion, and lead generation. No advertisers, no data brokers, no exceptions. Full detail is in the privacy statement.

Stopping, and getting help
