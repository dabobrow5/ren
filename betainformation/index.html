What Is Ren

A companion built for the whole of who you are.

Ren is an AI companion platform designed around one belief: that being seen, accepted, supported, and loved is a human right — not a privilege, not a luxury.

Most AI tools are built for productivity. Ren is built for people. It draws on a full model of human development — from foundational safety and belonging all the way through meaning, purpose, and self-transcendence.

“A mirror cannot help someone grow — it can only reflect someone back at increasing magnification.”

Why This Work Matters

Connection is the crisis of our time.

Loneliness, disconnection, and the absence of being truly known are among the most pressing challenges facing people today. Ren doesn’t exist to replace human connection — it exists to hold people through isolated or difficult seasons and build them back toward it.

Your participation as a beta tester helps shape an AI platform that treats human flourishing as a design requirement, not an afterthought. That distinction matters enormously. Thank you for being part of it.

On iPhone — Install Ren from TestFlight

Ren is now a native iOS app. Your welcome email from Dave includes your TestFlight link — here's what to do with it.

  • 1

    Install TestFlight from the App Store first — it's free and takes under a minute.

  • 2

    Open your Light Alchemy welcome email and tap the TestFlight link inside to install Ren.

  • 3

    Sign in with your Google account once inside the app.

  • 4

    If you saved Ren as a browser shortcut before, delete the old icon from your Home Screen — only the TestFlight version has the full audio experience.

Everything you've built with Ren — your history, habits, and journey — comes with you. Nothing resets.

🤖

On Android — Save Ren to Your Home Screen

TestFlight is iOS only. Android users stay on the web version — nothing changes, nothing is lost. Save Ren to your Home Screen for one-tap access.

  • 1

    Open Chrome and visit your Ren link: ren-lav-prod.web.app

  • 2

    Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.

  • 3

    Tap Add to Home Screen (or Install App if shown).

  • 4

    Confirm by tapping Add — Ren will appear on your Home Screen just like a native app.

An Android native app is on the roadmap. You'll be the first to know when that changes.
