Support

How to reach a person at Ren, and how to do the common things yourself.

If you need help right now

Ren is an AI companion, not a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911.

Write to us

support@lightalchemylabs.com. A person reads every message and answers within five business days. Inside the app, You › Settings also has a way to report a problem.

Your subscription

Ren is billed through Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Ren. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on Ren's subscription screen.

Deleting your account

In the app, open You › Settings › Delete my account. This removes your account and what Ren holds about you, as described in the privacy statement. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account, and deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in Settings as above.

Something someone sent you

On any Spark or Ripple you received, tap Report. You can also block a person from their page in Connect. Reports reach Ren's team, who review them within a day.
